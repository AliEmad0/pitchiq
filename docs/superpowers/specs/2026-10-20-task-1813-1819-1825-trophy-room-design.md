# TASK-1813 + TASK-1819 + TASK-1825 — Trophy Room and tactical style

**Date:** 2026-10-20 · **Owner said:** "start on 1819 and 1813 and 1825 together", then "just start" (no
question round). Every decision below that the owner did not make is marked **(my call)**.

## Why

The game now has eleven modes, a daily, seasons, post-match reports and What-If verdicts, and nothing
remembers any of it once a match ends. 1819 and 1813 give the coach somewhere that keeps score of a
career: the players he has fielded and the things he has done. 1825 gives him the one pre-match decision
the engine has supported since TASK-1805 but never offered: how his side plays.

## Goals

1. **Trophy Room** — one new page, `/game/trophies`, with two tabs: **Album** (1819) and **Hall of Fame** (1813).
2. **Album** — every card the coach fields in a finished match goes into his album automatically.
   Cards are grouped into **club-season sets** (Arsenal 2003/04, Man Utd 2007/08, …), each showing its
   progress. Completing a set turns it gold and unlocks a Hall of Fame badge.
3. **Hall of Fame** — retro achievements, evaluated by pure predicates when a match, a season, a daily or a
   What-If ends, and stored once unlocked. Locked ones show their condition, so the list is a list of goals.
4. **Tactical style** — a picker for the six styles on the draft screens of the modes that already play
   with styles. The pre-match screen shows the opponent's style and who has the edge.

## Non-goals and anti-goals

- **No backend, no account, no global ranking.** Everything is IndexedDB, per browser, like the daily.
- **No trading or crafting.** Duplicate counts are stored (so TASK-1829 can use them later) but not spent.
- **No engine change.** Styles already exist as a modifier; the album and Hall of Fame only read results.
- **Anti-goal: a choice that is not a choice.** If the coach could see the opponent's style while picking,
  he would always pick its counter and get +8 every match. The style is picked **before** the opponent's
  is revealed (the ticket's own shape: picker on the draft screen, edge on pre-match).
- **Anti-goal: a broken old link.** Every saved match and share link that exists today must replay exactly.
- **Anti-goal: Vercel cost.** Every new route is `force-static` with `revalidate = false`; no request-time work.

## Part 1 — Tactical style (TASK-1825)

**Owner (2026-10-20): "Scouting report".** The styles are a 5-cycle with Balanced neutral (`BEATS` in
`domain/opponent.ts`), so a blind pick against a uniformly random opponent is a coin flip: the same odds of
+8 as of −8. Instead each opponent has a **tendency** read from its own XI, the coach is shown a scouting
line ("They usually play Tiki-Taka"), and picks knowing it is likely but not certain. Reading the opponent
is the skill.

**Where the picker appears:** on the **pre-match screen** (`MatchProgramme`), the one screen every
style-playing mode already passes through and the only place the opponent is known in all of them (the
draft hub and the `PitchDraft` packs only draw the match seed at hand-off). This replaces the ticket's
"on the draft hub" — the ticket predates the owner's ruling and could not have scouting there. One shared
`StylePicker`, then **Kick off**.

**Modes:** `/game/draft` (H2H), `/game/chaos`, and the drafted packs (budget, chemistry, legacy, captains,
nation), **single-match format only**. **Hidden in season format (my call):** season fixtures keep playing
balanced-against-balanced as they do now, so showing a picker there would be a control that does nothing.
Also out (my call): the daily, Classic, Survival and What-If — none use styles today.

**The opponent's style (the new rule):**

- **Tendency** = a pure function of the opponent's XI (its mean attack / creation / defence / physical
  ratings → one of the six styles). Exact mapping is the builder's, pinned by a test over real squads that
  every style is some club's tendency.
- **Draw** = seeded from the same rng stream as today: the tendency with probability **0.6**, otherwise
  one of the other five uniformly (0.08 each). Revealed on the kick-off screen.
- The coach's **default** is **Balanced** (my call; no edge either way), or his last pick, remembered per
  browser as a convenience.

**Identity and old links (load-bearing):**

- The coach's style and the draw rule are part of the match's identity. They ride in `SavedMatch` and the
  share code, and reach `buildSession` through `RivalSetup` like `budget`/`chemistry` — every rebuild path
  (live, resume, share) or the fingerprint check fails.
- **Share code `v3`** carries the coach's style. A **`v2`** code (and a `SavedMatch` with no style) replays
  under the **old rule**: both styles from the uniform seeded draw, exactly what was played. So the draw is
  versioned, and the old path is pinned by the existing replay tests staying byte-identical.
- The session is built at hand-off as today; picking a style rebuilds it with the **same seed** at kick-off
  (style changes no squad, only the modifiers), so the opponent shown on the programme is the one played.

**The edge, in words:** after kick-off is pressed the programme names both styles and the result —
"Your High Press beats their Tiki-Taka", "Their Counter beats your High Press", or "No edge".
Strings in both catalogs; style names get Arabic names in `docs/i18n-glossary.md`.

## Part 2 — Album (TASK-1819)

**What gets collected (my call):** the cards in the coach's **XI** (plus a drafted bench where the mode has
one) when one of his matches **finishes** — every drafted single match, the daily, every coach fixture
in a Legacy-style season (played **or** simmed: the XI still turned out), and Classic and Survival
fixtures. **Not collected:** What-If (its XI is history's, not drafted) and **a shared link someone else
sent** (replaying another coach's match is not fielding those men; otherwise valid codes could be passed
around to farm cards and badges).

**Stored per card:** card id (`playerId@season`), name, role, club `teamId`, season, OVR, first-collected
day, and a **count** of finished matches he has played in. A match is recorded **once**: each recording
carries a match key (mode + seed + fixture), and a key already in the log is ignored. React runs effects
twice in development; without this the counts double.

**Sets (my call):** one per club-season — every **rated** card of that club's season squad, i.e. exactly
the pool Classic can field (`loadClassicSquads`), so every set is completable by playing that club's
Classic season. Measured before building (how many cards per set, and how many sets a Legacy pool can
reach). A mid-season transfer appears in both clubs' squads under one card id, so the album's key is
**card id + club** and fielding him for one club fills only that club's slot. The full list is too big to ship to
every page (18,126 cards), so it comes from a new prerendered static route,
`/api/game/album/[season]` (34 files, one per season, `force-static`, `dynamicParams = false`): for each club,
its card ids with name, role and OVR. The album fetches only the seasons the coach has cards in.

**The page:** progress overall, then the seasons he has cards in, then that season's clubs as sticker pages:
collected cards as stickers, missing ones as numbered empty slots (name hidden until collected: it is an
album). A finished set turns gold.

**Golden themes (ticket wording):** in this ticket a completed set gets a **gold page in the album and a
Hall of Fame badge**. Re-skinning the app's UI as a reward is left for a later ticket **(my call)**: it
would touch the Time-Machine era system, which is shared by the whole site.

## Part 3 — Hall of Fame (TASK-1813)

**Achievements (my call, 16 to start, each a pure predicate with a test):**

| Id               | Name              | Condition                                                              |
| ---------------- | ----------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| first-win        | Off the Mark      | Win any match                                                          |
| clean-sheet      | Shut Out          | Win without conceding                                                  |
| thrashing        | Thrashing         | Win by 5 or more                                                       |
| hat-trick        | Hat-trick Hero    | A player scores 3 in one match                                         |
| sparse-hat-trick | Old School        | A hat-trick by a 1992–2002 (sparse-era) card — reads `provenance.tier` |
| comeback         | Never Say Die     | Win after trailing by 2 or more                                        |
| late-winner      | Squeaky Bum Time  | Win with a goal at 90' or later that breaks a tie                      |
| ten-men          | Backs to the Wall | Win after one of your men is sent off                                  |
| champions        | Champions         | Win a league season (Legacy-style or Classic)                          |
| invincibles      | The Invincibles   | Finish a league season unbeaten                                        |
| great-escape     | Great Escape      | Survive in Survival                                                    |
| daily-7          | Seven Days        | A 7-day daily streak                                                   |
| rewrite          | History Rewritten | Rewrite any What-If                                                    |
| rewrite-all      | Alternate History | Rewrite all seven What-Ifs                                             |
| collector-100    | Collector         | 100 cards in the album                                                 |
| set-complete     | Full Set          | Complete a club-season set                                             |

**Rules:**

- Predicates live in `domain/`, pure, over what the end of a match already provides (`MatchResult.events`,
  the coach's side, the cards' provenance) or over a run's results. No engine change.
- Unlocked once, stored with the day and a short context ("Arsenal 2003/04 v Spurs, 5–0").
- **Backfill on first open (my call):** daily records, season / Classic / Survival saves and What-If bests
  already hold card ids and results, so the album and everything derivable from them (Seven Days, History
  Rewritten, Alternate History, Collector, Full Set, Champions / Invincibles for a finished run still saved)
  are filled in. Match-level badges cannot be backfilled: saved results carry no events.
- **Played vs simmed (my call):** match-level badges (first win, Shut Out, Thrashing, hat-tricks, comebacks,
  late winners, ten men) unlock only from matches the coach **played**, never from simmed fixtures, or bulk
  sim would farm them. Season-level badges read the whole run, simmed weeks included.
- **Which runs (my call):** Champions and Invincibles count full league runs (Legacy-style seasons and
  Classic). Survival plays only part of a season, so it has its own badge (Great Escape) and counts for
  neither.
- **Full Set is one badge (my call):** the Hall of Fame keeps a fixed list; it shows how many sets are
  complete, and each completed set is gold in the album.
- **Unlock moment:** the full-time screen shows a small "Achievement unlocked" banner for anything that
  match unlocked.

## Storage

`DB_VERSION` 4 → 5; new stores `album`, `trophies`, `trophyLog`. The upgrade is tested from a real v4
database. One best-effort entry point, `recordFullTime(entry)`, is called from every full-time path.
It never throws into a full-time screen; a failed write is logged and skipped, like `recordWhatIf`. The E2E
season helper now reads `DB_VERSION` (fixed in #222), so this bump does not repeat that failure.

## Navigation

`domain/modes.ts` already lists `album` and `hallOfFame` as planned collection surfaces under the mode gate.
They become live and link to `/game/trophies` (and `#hall`). `records` (TASK-1812) stays planned.

## Design ritual

Every game surface goes through the owner's gallery: 30 concepts as HTML files (not artifacts), playable
against real data, then a 20-design motion gallery, then build. Three surfaces: **Album**, **Hall of Fame**,
**Style picker + pre-match edge** (10 concepts each).

**Owner's picks (2026-10-21):** Album **#1 Panini Classic** (two-page sticker spread, numbered empty
slots, gold foil on a full set) · Hall of Fame **#1 Trophy Cabinet** (glass shelves, a cup per achievement,
lit once earned) · Style **#2 Scout's Dossier** (manila folder, CONFIDENTIAL stamp, the opponent's
style odds as bars, then the pick and Kick off). Gallery: `design/trophy-gallery/`.

**Motion (owner, 2026-10-21): hybrid #1 Sticker Slap + #5 Stamp Slam** (`design/trophy-gallery/motion.html`).
Each half owns different elements, so no element carries two animations (the TASK-1833 hybrid lesson):

- **Sticker Slap:** album stickers pop in with a small overshoot, staggered by slot; trophy-cabinet cups pop
  in the same way, staggered; the dossier's odds bars draw left to right (`scaleX`), staggered.
- **Stamp Slam:** the CONFIDENTIAL stamp slams down from 2.4× to rest at its −6° tilt; the folder then
  shakes once; the kick-off verdict slams in.
- Rules as always: only `transform` / `opacity` / `box-shadow`, every animation finite, one reduce gate
  covering every animated selector, and the guard test asserts RULES (`selector {` + `animation:`).
